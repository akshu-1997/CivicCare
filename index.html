About & Contact

⚠️ CivicCare is a project demonstration. It is not an official government service. For emergencies, contact your local emergency number or municipal helpline.

🏙️ About CivicCare

CivicCare is a public grievance and civic issue reporting system. Citizens report problems with roads, streetlights, drainage, waste and water supply, and everyone can see how those issues progress.

🎯 Project objective

Make reporting simple, make progress visible, and protect citizens' privacy while doing it.

🤝 How it helps citizens

Report in minutes with photo and GPS, get a unique ID, track status from Reported to Resolved, and browse issues in your neighbourhood.
